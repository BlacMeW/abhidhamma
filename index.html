Dedication

ဤအက်ပ်ကို သားသားလေး၏ ကောင်းကျိုး၊ စိတ်ငြိမ်သက်မှု၊ ပညာထွန်းကားမှုနှင့် မေတ္တာတရား တိုးပွားမှုအတွက် ရိုသေစွာ ရည်စူးပါသည်။

ဓမ္မလေ့လာသည့်အခါတိုင်း သတိပညာ ခိုင်မာစေပြီး၊ ရင်အေးသက်သာစေကာ၊ ဘဝလမ်းခရီးတွင် ကောင်းမွန်သည့် အားအင်နှင့် ငြိမ်းချမ်းမှုကို ပေးစွမ်းနိုင်သော နူးညံ့သည့် တရားအလှူတစ်ခုအဖြစ် ရှိနေစေလိုပါသည်။

For a beloved little son — always remembered, always loved.

In Memoriam

This software is a quiet tribute to Wunna Ko (June 5, 2010 – November 12, 2024) — a soul whose presence brought warmth, kindness, and quiet strength to those around him.

Son of Ko Wunna Ko and Myint Myint Thu, he was a beloved child whose gentle smile, thoughtful heart, and the love he shared continue to live on in the memories of family and friends.

Though his time with us was brief, the impact he made was lasting. His presence touched many lives, and his spirit remains a guiding light for those who knew him.

This application is created not to forget, but to remember — to hold a space where his spirit is felt, his voice is heard, and his legacy endures. Every element here reflects moments of who he was and what he meant to us.

This is not just software. It is a place of memory, of reflection, and of love.
